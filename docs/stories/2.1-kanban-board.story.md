## Story
**As a** Sales Agent,
**I want** to see my conversations in a Kanban board organized by stages,
**so that** I can manage my sales funnel visually with drag & drop.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Rota `/app/accounts/:id/crm` funcional e acessível pela sidebar.
2. Pinia store `crmPipeline` implementada com load/update de deals.
3. Componente `PipelineBoard` renderizando colunas dinamicamente com `vuedraggable`.
4. Drag & Drop entre colunas atualizando o backend (PATCH conversation).
5. Cards de deal (`DealCard`) exibindo nome do contato, status, assignee e labels.
6. Skeleton loading durante o carregamento inicial do board.
7. Seletor de pipeline no header funcional (caso haja múltiplos).
